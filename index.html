Walks from
the Door

Askrigg sits at the heart of Wensleydale — step outside and you're immediately surrounded by some of England's finest walking country.

0.5 mi

Mill Gill Force

A short stroll from the village to a beautiful hidden waterfall through ancient woodland. Perfect for all abilities.

Easy30 minWaterfall
2 mi

Whitfield Gill

Follow Whitfield Beck upstream through a dramatic limestone gorge, with views opening out across Wensleydale on the return.

Moderate2 hrsGorge
4 mi

Askrigg Common

Head up onto the open fell for sweeping panoramas of the dale. Wild moorland, dry stone walls and complete solitude.

Moderate3 hrsMoorland
6 mi

Aysgarth Falls

The iconic triple-tiered falls made famous by Robin Hood: Prince of Thieves. Walk the river path for the full spectacle.

Easy3 hrsWaterfall
8 mi

Hardraw Force

England's highest single-drop waterfall above ground. The walk through Hawes makes for a rewarding full day out.

Moderate4 hrsWaterfall
Cycling

Wensleydale Loop

The dale offers outstanding road cycling through quiet lanes and over dramatic passes. The Buttertubs Pass is unmissable for those who like a climb.

ChallengingVariableRoad cycling
