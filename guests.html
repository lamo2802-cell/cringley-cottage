Food & Drink

Local Recommendations

  • The Kings Arms, Askrigg — classic Dales pub, good food, very dog friendly. A short walk from the cottage.
  • The Crown Inn, Askrigg — another village favourite, relaxed atmosphere and local ales.
  • Bolton Arms, Aysgarth — lovely pub near the famous falls, great for a post-walk pint.
  • Wensleydale Creamery, Hawes — home of the famous Yorkshire cheese. Tours, tastings and a great café. Worth a visit.
  • Cockett's Hotel, Hawes — popular spot for lunch in Hawes town centre.
  • The Farmers Arms, Muker — a beautiful drive over the tops, excellent pub in a stunning village.
