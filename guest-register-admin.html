The register UK law asks us to keep (Immigration (Hotel Records) Order 1972): every guest aged 16 or over gives their name and nationality, and anyone who isn't British, Irish or a Commonwealth citizen also gives their passport or ID number, where it was issued, and where they're going next. Direct guests fill it in through the link in their booking confirmation email, with a reminder in the check-in day email if it's missing. Each stay's details are deleted automatically 12 months after checkout. If the police ask to see it, use "Download CSV".
