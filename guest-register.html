Guest registration

UK law asks every place that offers paid accommodation to keep a short register of guests aged 16 or over. Please fill in a line for each of them. It takes a minute.

British, Irish and Commonwealth citizens only need to give their name and nationality. For anyone else, the law also asks for their passport or ID card number and where they're going after their stay. We never need a photo or copy of your passport.

We keep these details for 12 months after your stay, as the law requires, and then delete them. Only Mark and Ruth can see them, and we'd only ever show them to the police if asked. More in our privacy notice.
